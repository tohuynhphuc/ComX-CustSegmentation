\section{Case Study Summary}

To formulate an effective solution for CompanyX, three industry benchmarks utilizing distinct segmentation methodologies were evaluated:

\subsection{Black Diamond (RFM Segmentation)}
Black Diamond, specializing in high-end technical climbing and outdoor equipment, transitioned from wholesale to Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) online [\textbf{Lexer2025}]. Faced with messy data across disparate systems, mass email marketing, and high customer churn, they established a Single Customer View (SCV) and applied Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) segmentation. Personalized email campaigns targeting inactive ($>180$ days) and lapsed ($>1$ year) customers yielded a +1,101\% revenue per email increase, a 50\% reduction in CPA, and a 2x Return on Advertising Spend (ROAS).

\subsection{Zappos (K-Means Clustering)}   
Zappos is an e-commerce platform for footwear and apparel [\textbf{TowardsAI2020}]. Facing uniform marketing inefficiencies, they deployed unsupervised K-Means clustering on Annual Income and Spending Score data. Using the Elbow Method, they identified 5 distinct customer groups (Target/VIPs, Savers, Spenders, Budget-conscious, and Standard), enabling tailored marketing campaigns and optimized outreach.

\subsection{DANA Indonesia (Data Warehouse + RFM + K-Means)} 
\textit{Corporate Profile:} Financial Technology (Fintech) digital wallet operating a B2C model. \textit{Business Pain Points:} Simple heuristic rules failed due to highly skewed transactional data, and they lacked an automated data warehouse architecture to daily ingest, standardize, score, and store customer segments. \textit{Solution:} Built an end-to-end customer segmentation pipeline utilizing Alibaba Cloud's MaxCompute (Data Warehouse) and Machine Learning Platform for AI (PAI), applying log transformations, K-Means clustering ($k=5$), and daily automated batch serving [\textbf{Pratama2020}]. \textit{Results:} Enabled seamless tracking of historical behavioral shifts over time and allowed marketing teams to query user segments directly via SQL and dashboards.

\section{Problem Statement}

Currently, CompanyX lacks a comprehensive, integrated customer analytics framework to track purchasing patterns and profile customer behaviors. Based on empirical transactional analysis, the primary strategic goal is to formulate a data-driven solution addressing three critical challenges:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Customer Retention \& Churn Mitigation:} How can CompanyX re-engage inactive customers and effectively retain the 62.9\% majority of one-time buyers who drop off after their initial order?
    \item \textbf{Cross-Selling Optimization:} How can we eliminate cross-selling inefficiencies and drive basket expansion for over 9,000 B2C customers who purchased bikes but never bought complementary accessories or apparel?
    \item \textbf{Data-Driven Segmentation Framework:} How can we establish a scalable Data Warehouse and machine learning pipeline leveraging K-Means Clustering to automatically segment CompanyX's customer base into distinct, actionable value tiers and behavioral profiles?
\end{itemize}
