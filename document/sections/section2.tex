\section{Customer Segmentation with RFM and K-Means}
RFM analysis summarizes purchasing behaviour using three variables: \textbf{Recency} (elapsed days since the most recent purchase), \textbf{Frequency} (number of orders), and \textbf{Monetary} value (amount spent). An RFM profile makes customer behaviour measurable without relying on arbitrary demographic categories. In this project, frequency and monetary values are calculated using a trailing 365-day window, while recency is measured relative to the observation date. The definitions and preprocessing are described in Chapter~\ref{chap:ml}.

Traditional RFM segmentation commonly places customers into groups according to hand-written thresholds or ranks. This can be transparent and inexpensive, but the boundaries must be selected in advance. K-Means provides an alternative: customers are grouped according to their positions in a numeric feature space, using distances to cluster centroids. For this project, the feature space is the log-transformed and standardized RFM vector. K-Means does not automatically supply business labels; the clusters must be interpreted using their purchasing statistics.

The segmentation model alone is not sufficient for operational business intelligence. A \emph{data warehouse} provides consistent customer keys, historical demographic versions, RFM snapshots, and persisted model configurations. SQL Server Integration Services (SSIS) performs incremental extraction and loading, while Power BI connects the results to decision-making dashboards. This combination is the central design choice of CompanyX.

\section{Related Industry Examples}
\subsection{Black Diamond: Data-Driven Retention}
The outdoor-equipment brand Black Diamond worked with customer-data platform provider Lexer to consolidate customer information and improve targeted acquisition and retention activities. According to the provider's case study, separate initiatives achieved a 50\% reduction in acquisition cost and a 1,101\% increase in revenue per email for a lapsed-customer campaign \parencite{lexer2019blackdiamond}. These are externally reported business outcomes, \emph{not} results of the CompanyX implementation. They illustrate why identifying inactive and valuable customers may support differentiated actions.

\subsection{K-Means as a Customer Segmentation Method}
A published customer-segmentation tutorial presents K-Means as a way to form groups from numerical customer features \parencite{towardsai2022kmeans}. Its general lesson is relevant here: numerical scaling, cluster-number selection, and cluster interpretation are all necessary before the grouping is useful for marketing. A common demonstration uses annual income and a spending score, whereas \emph{CompanyX uses RFM transaction features}. We do not treat the tutorial as evidence of an internal deployment by a particular retailer.

\subsection{Alibaba Cloud: An End-to-End Segmentation Example}
Pratama's Alibaba Cloud tutorial demonstrates a pipeline for customer segmentation using data preparation, MaxCompute storage, K-Means training, and model serving \parencite{pratama2020segmentation}. The tutorial uses a public retail dataset as an example; it is \emph{not evidence} that the author's employer, DANA Indonesia, operated the same pipeline in production. For CompanyX, the relevant principle is linking the data pipeline with a repeatable training and scoring workflow, rather than running clustering once in an isolated notebook.

\section{Problem Statement and Chosen Solution}
CompanyX needs an integrated process for answering three business questions: \emph{Which customers are most valuable? Which customers show reduced engagement? How can the company tailor its actions to distinct purchasing behaviours?} The team addresses them with five RFM-based clusters, two complementary fact tables, and an automated path from operational records to a Power BI dashboard. The resulting segments provide an analytical basis for recommendations; proving that a proposed marketing campaign increases revenue or reduces churn would require a separate intervention study.
