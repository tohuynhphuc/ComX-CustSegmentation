\chapter{Introduction \& Problem Statement}
\section{Context}

In retail and e-commerce enterprise systems, understanding customer purchasing patterns is vital for sustainable growth. Analysis of initial transactional data at CompanyX revealed four core business pain points:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{High Single-Purchase Churn:} 62.9\% of customers make only one order and never return. Only 29.5\% make a second order, and a minimal 7.4\% complete 3 to 5 orders.
    \item \textbf{Basket Size Limitations:} A significant portion of transactions involve minimal item counts (2,492 single-item purchases vs. 4,480 two-item purchases), indicating low initial basket size expansion.
    \item \textbf{Cross-Selling Inefficiencies:} Over 9,000 B2C customers purchased products in the ``Bikes'' category but never bought complementary items from ``Accessories'' or ``Clothing''.
    \item \textbf{Customer Inactivity:} A high proportion of customer profiles remain inactive for extended durations ($>180$ days), reducing total Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) and inflating Customer Acquisition Costs (CPA).
\end{itemize}
